Frequently asked

Is everything on Campus Connect free?+

Yes — every resource, event listing and support contact is free to browse and use.

Do I need to sign up?+

No account is required to browse. The contact form only asks for what's needed to follow up with you.

How often is content updated?+

Resources and events are reviewed weekly so links stay current and listings stay relevant.

Can I suggest a resource?+

Absolutely — use the contact form and select "Suggest a resource" from the topic list.

Can't find what you're looking for?

Send us a message and we'll point you to the right person or resource.
