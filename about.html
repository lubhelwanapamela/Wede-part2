Who we are

Built by students who got tired of searching in ten different places.

Campus Connect started as a simple idea: put everything a student needs — support, resources, events and help — behind one link.

Our mission

Quick access to what matters, without the runaround.

Campus Connect's mission is to give students fast, reliable access to helpful tools and information, so they can enjoy their time as students and achieve academic success — without losing hours to scattered pages, group chats and outdated flyers.

Our vision

A trusted digital home for every student

We want Campus Connect to grow into a dependable online resource where students can quickly locate information, connect with one another, and get help whenever they need it — on any device, at any hour.

Who it's for

Made for students navigating everything at once

Our primary audience is college and university students, particularly those aged 18–30, along with new students who need help finding their footing on campus.

First-year students

Finding orientation info, key dates and where to go for help, without asking ten different people.

Returning students

Staying on top of deadlines, tutoring slots and events without digging through old emails.

Student organisers

Getting society events and meetups seen, without competing for space on ten social feeds.

Why we redesigned

From cluttered and hard to navigate, to clear and student-first

The earlier version of our site had useful information, but it was easy to lose: dense pages, inconsistent navigation and a layout that didn't adapt well to phones. We kept what worked — direct, relevant content — and rebuilt the rest around clarity.

✓Clear navigation ✓Mobile-first layout ✓Modern visual design ✓Faster to scan

Research

We looked at what students actually struggled to find on the old site.

Restructure

Content was grouped into four clear areas: academic, events, wellbeing and contact.

Redesign

A calmer colour palette, bigger touch targets and a layout that works on any screen.

Test & refine

We keep an eye on what students click, search and skip — and adjust accordingly.
